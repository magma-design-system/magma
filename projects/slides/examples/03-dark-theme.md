# Dark by default

`theme: maggioli-dark` forces the deck into dark mode.

---
layout: content
title: How dark mode works
---

Nothing is re-styled per slide. Setting the theme adds a
`pref-theme-scheme-dark` class on the root, and Magma's global `--tone-*`
tokens flip. Every layout follows automatically.

---
layout: quote
---

> The same content, the same tokens - light or dark, unmistakably Magma.
