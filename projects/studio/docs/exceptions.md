# Exceptions

Stage 4: how a function shared by several components paints in every state, on every ground (the
band of tabs and filters, the track of a quantity, the frame of the fields). It waits for Magma 3.

Address: `#/exceptions`.

**Exceptions** (`src/features/exceptions/`, #1402) has no view yet: the order of the stages holds
a page that says what it will hold and when it comes, and leads on to the
[full review](full-review.md). It comes after Magma 3 (vittorio, 2026-11-27, #1404): its
functions, the band, the track and the frame of the fields, are roles of Magma 3 (`transparent`,
`fieldRoles` of `semantic.config.ts` on the branch `1292-magma-3`), whose weights enter the preset
with them, and its candidates need an engine that generates and measures them; the object model
puts the exceptions after the first release too (section 1). Then the stage holds the functions,
the components that adopt them, their candidates and the weight they share with
[Weights](weights.md). The page is written in the classes of Magma and needs no sheet of its own.
